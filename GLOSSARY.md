# mini-trace

mini-trace makes coding-agent activity observable and searchable.

## Language

**Reported cost**:
The cost supplied by the coding agent or its telemetry source. For OpenCode, this is not necessarily the amount billed.

**Estimated cost**:
A calculated approximation of cost based on available usage and pricing information. It is distinct from reported cost and is not necessarily the amount billed.

**Session tree**:
A coding-agent session and the subagent sessions whose parent relationships are known. Its usage total includes the root session and its descendants.
